How the numbers work

  • Money is credited only after an ad network actually pays for a month, never from estimates.
  • Half of that goes to the people whose views earned it. Referral cuts come out of our half, never theirs.
  • Cash goes to PayPal once someone has $10, after a 30-day hold on their first cash-out.
  • Donations go out monthly, and are marked paid here once sent.
  • The Claude Code mod is open source, so you can check exactly what it sends.
