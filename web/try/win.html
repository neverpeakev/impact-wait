What Vibedough sees

  • The first 500 characters of your prompt, so the ad fits what you're working on.
  • Whether you're at the keyboard, so views only count when a person is there.

What it never sees

  • Your files or your code.
  • Tool output, terminal output or anything the agent writes.
  • Anything at all from subagents running in the background.

Questions

What's the prize?

One winner gets $1,000. Entries come from paid views, plus a free way to enter that doesn't need the app. Official rules, dates and eligibility are published before the draw opens. No purchase necessary to enter or win.

Does it slow Claude Code down?

No. The ad loads alongside the agent's work and never holds up a reply. If an ad is slow to arrive, you simply don't see one.

Do I have to click the ads?

No, and please don't click ones you don't care about. Views count, clicks don't.

Can I turn it off?

Yes, with one command, any time. Turn it back on the same way.

What about Cursor, Codex and the others?

Claude Code comes first. Tell us which tools you use after you sign up, and that decides what's next.
